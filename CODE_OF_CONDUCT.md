# Code of Conduct

This is a small infrastructure-documentation project. The expectation is
simple: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on
the technical content.

- Be constructive in issues and PR reviews — critique the config/doc, not
  the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks, in this repo's
  issues, PRs, or discussions.
- Stay on topic: this repo is about the networking/infrastructure pattern
  it documents, not a general support forum for unrelated products.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be made via a GitHub issue marked
clearly as a conduct concern, or directly to the maintainer. Maintainers
may remove comments, commits, or contributions that don't follow this,
and may block repeat offenders.

This is a lightweight adaptation of the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) scaled to
this project's size.
